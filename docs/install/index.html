Install on the FM-1

Over USB, from this page, in Chrome or Edge. Each is its latest release, checked against GitHub's SHA-256 of it and its own checksums before anything is sent. Each is a whole firmware: installing one replaces the one on the FM-1. Back to any other, or to the official one, with the FM-1 Firmware Hub.

A Commodore 64 SID on the FM-1: C64 tunes on a 6510 and reSID, a GoatTracker 2 editor and a SID synth. More. As a firmware of its own here, or added to fm1-dock (above).
